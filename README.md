# AZAI Trade Launch

Create a modern, professional, responsive one-page website for AZAI Trade, a Trucking & Dispatching Company.

Reference website for overall structure and business style:
https://www.eagledispatchers.com

Use the reference only for inspiration. Do NOT copy its branding, logo, text, images, or exact design.

## COMPANY

Company Name: AZAI Trade
Business: Trucking & Dispatching Company
Phone: 347-988-6771
Email: Iskai2773@gmail.com

The website must make it immediately obvious that AZAI Trade is a trucking and truck dispatching company.

When a visitor lands on the website, they should immediately see visual and textual references to:
- Semi trucks
- Truck drivers
- Freight
- Truck dispatching
- Owner-operators
- Carriers
- Loads

Do NOT make the website look like a generic logistics, consulting, or transportation company.

---

# WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Create a single-page website with these sections:
Home | Services | About | Get Started

Use smooth scrolling between sections.

---

# 1. HOME / HERO

The hero section is extremely important.
Use a large, professional semi-truck / tractor-trailer image as the main visual.
The image should clearly show a real commercial truck, preferably on a highway or delivering freight.

The hero should immediately communicate:
AZAI Trade
Trucking & Dispatching

Suggested headline:
"Keep Your Truck Moving. We Handle the Dispatch."

Supporting text:
"Professional truck dispatching and freight support for owner-operators and carriers. We help you find loads, negotiate rates, and stay focused on the road."

Primary CTA:
GET STARTED

Secondary CTA:
OUR SERVICES

Add small trust/industry labels below the buttons:
Truck Dispatching • Freight • Owner-Operators • Carriers

The first screen should feel like a real trucking company website.

---

# 2. SERVICES

Section heading:
"Our Trucking & Dispatching Services"

Short description:
"From finding the right loads to managing the details behind every trip, AZAI Trade helps keep your truck moving and your business running."

Create professional service cards:
- Truck Dispatching: Professional dispatching services for owner-operators and trucking companies.
- Load Finding: We search for available freight and help connect you with loads that fit your truck and preferred lanes.
- Rate Negotiation: We communicate with brokers and work to negotiate competitive rates for your loads.
- Route & Trip Planning: Plan efficient routes and trips while helping reduce unnecessary empty miles.
- Freight & Broker Communication: Professional communication with brokers and shippers throughout the load process.
- Paperwork & Administrative Support: Help with rate confirmations, load information, and other dispatch-related documentation.

Make the service cards visually connected to the trucking industry.
Use subtle icons (semi truck, freight/load, route/map, phone/communication, documents, dollar/rate).

---

# 3. ABOUT

Section heading:
"Built to Keep Your Truck Moving"

Explain that AZAI Trade provides trucking dispatching support for owner-operators and carriers.

Suggested copy:
"At AZAI Trade, we understand that being on the road is more than just driving. Finding good freight, communicating with brokers, negotiating rates, and managing paperwork can take valuable time away from your business.

Our goal is simple: help truck drivers and carriers spend more time moving freight while we handle the dispatching side of the operation."

Highlight:
- Professional dispatching
- Reliable communication
- Load search support
- Rate negotiation
- Driver-focused service
- Long-term business relationships

Add a large professional image of a truck driver or semi-truck.

---

# 4. GET STARTED

Create a strong CTA section.
Headline:
"Ready to Put Your Truck to Work?"

Supporting text:
"Tell us about yourself and your truck. Complete the form and the AZAI Trade team will contact you about getting started."

Add a large button:
GET STARTED

When the visitor clicks GET STARTED, open a professional application form (clean modern modal on desktop, comfortable modal/form on mobile).

# VISUAL IDENTITY

The website must visually communicate:
TRUCKING + DISPATCHING + PROFESSIONAL BUSINESS

Use a strong, modern trucking aesthetic.
Recommended colors: Dark navy / deep blue, charcoal / black, white, light gray, one professional accent color.
Avoid neon colors, acid/bright colors, childish designs, generic corporate stock imagery.
Primary visual identity must be TRUCKS (semi-trucks, tractor trailers, drivers, highways, freight).
